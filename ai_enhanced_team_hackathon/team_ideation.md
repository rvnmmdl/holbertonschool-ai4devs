# Team Ideation

## Brainstormed Ideas
1. AI Study Planner – builds a personalized weekly study schedule from a student's courses, deadlines and available time
2. AI Code Review Buddy – explains pull request changes in plain language and flags likely bugs
3. AI Meeting Summarizer – turns meeting notes or transcripts into summaries with action items
4. AI Bug Triage Assistant ✅ (chosen) – classifies bug reports by severity and suggests likely causes and next steps

## Problem Statement
Development teams spend a lot of time reading, sorting and prioritizing incoming bug reports. Reports are often vague, duplicated or incorrectly prioritized, which slows down fixes. An AI assistant can turn a raw bug report into a structured triage result (severity, category, probable cause, suggested next steps) so the team can focus on fixing instead of sorting.

## MVP Features
- Input form for a raw bug report (title, description, steps to reproduce)
- AI classifies severity (Low / Medium / High / Critical) and category (UI, backend, performance, security, etc.)
- AI suggests probable root causes and recommended next steps
- Detection of possible duplicate reports among previously submitted ones
- Simple dashboard listing triaged bugs, sortable by severity
