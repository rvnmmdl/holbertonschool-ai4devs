# Product Idea: Carpool Connect

## Vision
Make daily commuting more affordable, convenient, and sustainable by helping people traveling in the same direction find and share trusted rides.

## Primary Users and Their Needs

- **Drivers:** Want to share empty seats, offset fuel and parking costs, and find passengers whose routes and schedules fit their own.
- **Passengers:** Want reliable, affordable transportation, predictable pickup times, and a safe way to travel with other commuters.
- **Employers and Universities:** Want to reduce parking demand, support employees' and students' commutes, and encourage more sustainable travel.

## Key Features

- **Route and Schedule Matching:** Match drivers and passengers based on pickup points, destinations, departure times, and available seats.
- **Recurring Commutes:** Let users create regular weekly trips and automatically find compatible ride partners.
- **In-App Messaging:** Allow matched users to coordinate pickup locations, timing, and trip details.
- **Cost Sharing:** Estimate each passenger's contribution and support transparent expense splitting.
- **Profiles, Ratings, and Verification:** Build trust through user profiles, trip history, ratings, and optional identity or organization verification.
- **Live Trip Updates:** Share trip status and notify participants about delays, cancellations, or arrival.
- **Safety Tools:** Provide trip sharing with trusted contacts, emergency contact information, and clear reporting options.
- **Environmental Impact Tracking:** Show users the estimated money saved, distance shared, and emissions avoided.

## Differentiators

- **Commute-first matching:** Prioritizes repeated home-to-work or home-to-campus journeys rather than one-off trips.
- **Schedule flexibility:** Supports recurring schedules while allowing users to update individual days when plans change.
- **Trust-focused community:** Combines verification options, ratings, and trip-sharing safety features.
- **Transparent savings and impact:** Makes financial and environmental benefits visible after each trip.
- **Organization communities:** Allows employers and universities to create private commuting groups for people who share a workplace or campus.

## Product Goal
Help commuters spend less on transportation, make better use of available vehicle seats, and reduce the number of single-occupancy car trips.
